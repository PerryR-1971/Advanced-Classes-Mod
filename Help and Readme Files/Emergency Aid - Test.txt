Emergency Aid - test ability

Script: Data/s/zz acm emergency aid test.erm
During a normal First Aid Tent turn, CTRL + left click a valid allied healing target.
Restores up to floor(remaining Heal Capacity / 2) HP and sets that side's entire Heal Capacity to zero.
The target cannot exceed its original creature count. First Aid is required to resurrect. Without it, only the top creature is healed.
Summoned and cloned stacks are excluded. The normal heal action is replaced, not added.
The ability does not repeat Overheal, Herbalism or Treatment rewards.
Battle log: actual HP, revived creatures, full capacity spent and 50% efficiency.
Arrow Volley already logs activation and final arrow count.

Manual tests required:
- 1000 capacity, >=500 missing HP: heal 500, capacity 0.
- 1000 capacity, 100 missing HP: heal 100, capacity 0.
- No First Aid: no resurrection.
- Odd capacity: usable amount rounded down.
- Both sides, CTRL versus ordinary click, replay and next battle.
- With Tent upgrades: confirm no additional normal heal or bonus rewards.
- Arrow Volley and Emergency Aid together: only the active machine's ability triggers.

Prototype: not executed in the game yet.
Remove the corresponding .erm to disable the test ability.
