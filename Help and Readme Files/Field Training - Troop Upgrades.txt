FIELD TRAINING - TROOP UPGRADE SYSTEM
=====================================

OVERVIEW
--------
Field Training is the troop-development counterpart to Spell Training. It allows
heroes to permanently improve creature families in their own army.

Open the Field Training window by right-clicking an occupied troop slot in the
hero screen. Warrior, Adventurer and Mage class heroes can use the system.
Right-click an upgrade button or its text for detailed rules, its maximum effect
and the total Gold cost required to reach its fixed maximum level.


TRAINING CAPACITY
-----------------
Each purchased upgrade level consumes one training point.

Mage:        1 starting point, +1 point every 5 hero levels
Adventurer:  3 starting points, +1 point every 3 hero levels
Warrior:     5 starting points, +1 point every 2 hero levels

Economy specialists keep the starting points of their class, receive 10 extra
starting points and gain 1 point per hero level instead of the normal class
growth. Five Mithril can be exchanged for one permanent additional point.


COSTS AND LIMITS
----------------
Gold cost for the next upgrade level: 1,000 x level^2 + 5,000.

Next level:       1        5         10         15          20
Level cost:   6,000   30,000    105,000    230,000     405,000
Total cost:   6,000   80,000    435,000  1,315,000   2,970,000

- Stack size does not affect the price.
- An upgrade cannot exceed the hero's current level or its own maximum level.
- A creature family may receive several different upgrades.
- Upgrades belong to the combination of hero and creature family.
- Base and upgraded creatures share their training. Splitting or moving a stack
  within the same hero's army therefore does not remove its improvements.


AVAILABLE UPGRADES
------------------
Extra Strike             +1% chance per level                         Max. 15
Critical Chance          +1.5% chance per level                       Max. 15
Critical Damage          +2% critical damage per level                Max. 20
Block                     +2% chance to halve physical damage          Max. 15
Flat Damage Reduction     Hero level x upgrade level less damage       Max. 15
Flat Damage               Hero level x upgrade level more damage       Max. 20
Damage vs. Ranged         +2% damage per level against shooters        Max. 15
Damage vs. Melee          +1% damage per level against non-shooters    Max. 15
Damage vs. Undead/Elem.   +3% damage per level                         Max. 15
Combat Speed              +4% speed per level, normally rounded        Max. 10
Crushing Blow             +1% chance per level                         Max. 20
Life Leech                +0.5% life leech per level                    Max. 10
Act Again                 +1% chance per level                          Max. 10
Act First                 +3% chance per level                          Max. 20

Crushing Blow reduces the target's Attack and Defense by 2 and Speed by 1.
Act Again can trigger at most once per stack and combat round.
Act First grants +50 Speed during the first combat round only.
