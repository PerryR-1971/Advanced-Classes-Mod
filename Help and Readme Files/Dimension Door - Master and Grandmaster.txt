Dimension Door: Master and Grandmaster

Human adventure-map casting:
- None/Basic/Advanced: 1 cast per hero per day, 40 SP, 300 movement per cast.
- Expert: 2 casts per hero per day, 40 SP each, 300 movement per cast.
- Master: 2 casts per hero per day, 40/60 SP, 200 movement per cast.
- Grandmaster: 3 casts per hero per day, 40/60/100 SP, 100 movement per cast.
- These are the final spell-table costs, without ACM's Sorcery surcharge.
- The school is read from the spell data (Water Magic in ACM).
- Range and destination restrictions are unchanged.
- The native rule allowing a cast with positive movement and clamping the
  remaining movement to zero is preserved.
- The native daily counter is used, including casts made before a mastery upgrade.
- Closing the book, switching heroes and leaving the cast restores spell values.

Implementation uses MG0/MG1, which ERA exposes for human players only.
AI casting retains its existing behavior.

In-game verification still required:
1. None/Basic/Advanced: 40 SP and 300 movement; Expert: 40 SP and 300 movement
   for each of its two casts. No cost escalation below Master.
2. Master: spend 40, then 60 SP and 200 movement per cast; no third cast.
3. Grandmaster: spend 40/60/100 SP and 100 movement per cast; no fourth cast.
4. On Master/GM, with 59 SP before cast 2 or 99 SP before cast 3, casting is
   unavailable. On Expert, cast 2 costs only 40 SP.
5. Cancel the spellbook or target selection: no extra cast or SP is consumed.
6. Switch heroes: costs, descriptions and daily limits match the new hero.
7. Save/load after cast 1: Expert stays at 40 SP; Master/GM stays at 60 SP.
8. Next day: first cast costs 40 SP again; another hero has an independent count.
9. Check opening the book from the hero screen and visiting a mage guild.

Fly (human casting):
- Movement per cast: None 250, Basic 200, Advanced 150, Expert 100, Master 50,
  Grandmaster 0. The current flight effect and spell-point costs are retained.
- Base SP costs: 32/28/24/20/20/20. ACM's spellbook Sorcery surcharge is unchanged.
- Insufficient movement blocks casting before the Fly effect or SP deduction.
- Test each rank with exact movement, one point too few, and zero on Grandmaster.
- Casting again while Fly is already active keeps the native behavior.
